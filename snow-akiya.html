Questions

Can foreigners buy houses in Japan?

Yes. Japan places very few limits on foreign buyers (a few areas near sensitive facilities need a notification). Owning a house doesn't come with a visa.

Are you a real estate agent?

No. We're a newsletter. We don't broker deals or take commissions.
